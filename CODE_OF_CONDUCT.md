# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Refraining from demeaning or discriminatory comments.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Inappropriate language or personal attacks.
- Posting private information without consent.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the repository maintainers.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and community channels.
